Three in a Row

Place three marks in a row before a tactical, intentionally beatable computer opponent.

Play

Use the mouse or touch to select a board cell. Arrow keys move focus, Enter places a mark, F1 opens help, and Escape closes help or exits. Completed wins, draws, and losses are saved locally.
